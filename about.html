ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Fathi Academic Knowledge Archive

A personal academic knowledge infrastructure designed to organise, connect and preserve research, academic materials and scholarly resources.

PURPOSE

Why This Archive Exists

Organise

Academic materials are organised through structured categories, document types, topics, keywords and metadata.

Connect

Knowledge can be connected across disciplines, concepts, institutions, legal instruments, research themes and related materials.

Preserve

The archive provides a structured digital space for preserving academic work, research materials and scholarly references.

KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE

Built as an Academic Knowledge System

Structured Data

Academic materials are represented using structured metadata rather than relying only on manually organised pages.

Search & Discovery

Search, filtering and categorisation are designed to make academic knowledge easier to discover.

Research Infrastructure

The architecture is designed to grow from a personal repository into a larger research and knowledge platform.

KNOWLEDGE DOMAINS

Areas of Focus

International Relations

International politics, foreign policy, international institutions and global affairs.

International Law

Treaties, legal principles, international institutions and international jurisprudence.

Human Rights

Human rights law, mechanisms, institutions and contemporary human rights issues.

International Security

Conflict, strategic affairs, peace and security within the international system.

International Humanitarian Law

Legal frameworks governing armed conflict and the protection of persons affected by war.

International Criminal Law

International crimes, individual criminal responsibility and international criminal justice.

ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLE

Complexity in the Architecture. Simplicity in the Experience.

The archive is designed so that increasingly sophisticated data structures, search systems, relationships and research tools can operate behind a clear and accessible interface.
